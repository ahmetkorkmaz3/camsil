## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-09

### Changed

- The spray is a mist that flies from the nozzle to the glass. The droplets form where the mist lands, over about 0.2 seconds.
- The cloth is a yellow sponge cloth. It bends and wrinkles when you wipe, and it has a soft shadow.
- The bottle is larger and moves like a bottle in your hand. It follows the cursor with a small delay, tilts when you move it, and kicks back when you spray.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-09

The first public release.

### Added

- The main screen shows as a dirty window: dust, water spots, and fingerprints.
- Spray bottle: a left click sprays water. The droplets run down the glass and dry in about 8 seconds.
- Cloth: wipe wet glass to clean it. Dry wiping only spreads the dust. The squeak changes pitch with the speed.
- Right click or Space changes the tool. The 1 and 2 keys also select a tool.
- The progress shows on the screen. At 95% clean, the glass shines and Camsil closes.
- Esc or Cmd+Q closes Camsil at any time. Camsil also closes after 2 minutes without input.
- Install and update with one command: `install.sh`.
